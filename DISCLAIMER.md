## 3. Purpose, Digital Preservation & Legal Basis

- **Digital Archival & Preservation:** The primary purpose of this repository is digital preservation, historical documentation, educational research, interoperability analysis, and hardware recovery / right-to-repair support.
- **Right to Repair & Electronic Waste (E-Waste) Reduction:**
  - **Preventing Artificial Obsolescence:** Greek ISPs routinely deploy hundreds of thousands of CPE units that become locked or abandoned once contracts terminate, service tiers upgrade, or remote TR-069 management ceases. Without accessible firmware and recovery tooling, functional devices are rendered unusable and discarded.
  - **Hardware Repurposing:** This repository supports extending the lifecycle of legacy and decommissioned equipment by facilitating their recovery and repurposing as standalone Wi-Fi access points, managed Ethernet switches, ATA/VoIP adapters, or home lab network testbeds.
  - **EU Sustainability Directives & Circular Economy:** In accordance with the objectives of **Directive (EU) 2024/1799 (Right to Repair)**, **Directive 2012/19/EU (WEEE - Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment)**, and the **EU Circular Economy Action Plan**, facilitating the repair, reuse, and repurposing of consumer-owned or discarded hardware actively reduces toxic electronic waste and resource consumption.
- **European Union Legal Exceptions & Greek Law:** In accordance with the principles set forth in **Directive 2009/24/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council** (specifically Articles 5 and 6 regarding backup copies, decompilation for interoperability, and error correction) and its transposition into Greek Law under **Law 2121/1993 (Articles 42 & 43)**, this archive facilitates interoperability and continued operation of legally acquired networking hardware.
- **Non-Commercial Nature:** This repository is operated on a strictly non-profit and non-commercial basis. No monetary compensation, subscription fees, advertising income, donations, or commercial gains are solicited or accepted in connection with this project.
- **No Circumvention / Anti-Piracy Notice:** This repository archives unmodified stock or factory firmware binaries. It does **not** provide tools, encryption keys, or exploits designed to circumvent digital rights management (DRM), defeat access controls to paywalled services, clone subscriber identities, steal telecommunications bandwidth, or disrupt carrier network infrastructure.
